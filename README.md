# Hackathon-FoodBridge
Unit project 
FoodBridge 

Connecting Surplus Food with People in Need

 About the Project

FoodBridge is a social initiative designed to reduce food waste and help fight hunger by connecting large companies with organizations that prepare and distribute meals to people in need.

Every day, businesses such as supermarkets, restaurants, hotels, and food producers may have surplus food that is still safe to consume but would otherwise be discarded. At the same time, many people struggle to access nutritious meals.

FoodBridge aims to bridge this gap by creating a system that collects suitable surplus food, transports it to designated preparation centers, and transforms it into meals that can be safely distributed to vulnerable communities.

The Problem

1. Food Waste

Large companies often discard edible food because of excess inventory, unsold products, or logistical limitations. When food that is still safe to consume is thrown away, valuable resources such as water, energy, land, and labor are wasted as well.

2. Hunger and Food Insecurity

Millions of people around the world face difficulties accessing sufficient and nutritious food. Although edible surplus food exists, it does not always reach the communities that need it most.

The Main Challenge

The problem is not only the existence of food waste or hunger, but also the lack of effective systems to connect surplus food with people who need it.

The Solution

FoodBridge proposes a coordinated food recovery and redistribution system that connects businesses with food preparation centers, nonprofit organizations, and communities in need.

Instead of discarding suitable surplus food, participating companies would notify the system when food is available. The food would then be collected, inspected, and transported to authorized preparation centers, where it could be handled and prepared safely before being distributed through partner organizations.

How It Works

1. Food Registration: Participating companies report available surplus food, including its type, quantity, condition, and collection deadline.
2. Food Assessment: The food is checked to ensure that it meets applicable food safety requirements. Unsafe or unsuitable food is rejected.
3. Collection and Transportation: Coordinated partners collect and transport eligible food under appropriate conditions.
4. Meal Preparation: Qualified staff prepare meals at designated facilities following food safety and hygiene standards.
5. Distribution: Partner organizations deliver the prepared meals to people and communities experiencing food insecurity.
6. Impact Tracking: The system records the amount of food recovered, meals distributed, and waste potentially prevented.

Main Objectives

* Reduce the amount of edible food discarded by large companies.
* Increase access to prepared meals for people experiencing food insecurity.
* Connect businesses, food preparation centers, and nonprofit organizations.
* Promote responsible food management and community participation.
* Measure the social and environmental impact of food recovery.

Who Benefits?

* People in need: Gain access to meals and food assistance.
* Businesses: Find a responsible alternative to discarding eligible surplus food.
* Nonprofit organizations: Receive support to serve their communities.
* The environment: Benefits from preventing avoidable food waste.

Expected Impact

FoodBridge aims to contribute to:

* Less edible food going to waste.
* More meals reaching people who need them.
* Better coordination between businesses and community organizations.
* Greater awareness of food waste and food insecurity.

The project’s impact will be evaluated using measurable indicators, such as kilograms of food recovered, meals distributed, participating businesses, and people reached.

Food Safety and Responsibility

Food safety is a fundamental part of the project. Only food that meets applicable safety requirements will be accepted. Collection, transportation, storage, preparation, and distribution must follow local regulations and appropriate hygiene and temperature-control procedures.

The project will prioritize collaboration with qualified food handlers and authorized organizations.

Future Development

Future versions of FoodBridge could include:

* A digital platform for businesses to register surplus food.
* A map of collection points and preparation centers.
* Notifications for available food and collection deadlines.
* A coordination system for volunteers and partner organizations.
* A dashboard to visualize food recovery and social impact.

How to Contribute

FoodBridge welcomes collaboration from people interested in reducing food waste and improving access to food.

Potential partners include supermarkets, restaurants, food producers, nonprofit organizations, community kitchens, logistics providers, and volunteers.

Our Mission

To turn surplus food into an opportunity to nourish people, reduce waste, and build more sustainable communities.

Food should feed people, not end up in the trash.
